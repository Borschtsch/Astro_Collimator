Phone camera

Keep your phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. No internet connection is needed.

Checking camera zoom…

Open the browser camera to take a photo or send video. You can also choose an existing photo.

Browser photos and video need HTTPS and camera permission. Choose photo can send an existing file without camera access.

Connection and certificate details

Certificates are generated on your computer. Nothing is installed automatically. If your browser accepts the local HTTPS connection, try video without installation. Some phones require a trusted certificate for camera access.

Before optionally trusting a certificate, open Compare certificates in the computer's Camera tab. Match all SHA-256 digits. A matching number on this page alone is not proof: also check the certificate details shown by your browser or phone's installation screen.

Optional iPhone/iPad certificate profile

Install only if you choose to trust this computer and your phone needs it.
